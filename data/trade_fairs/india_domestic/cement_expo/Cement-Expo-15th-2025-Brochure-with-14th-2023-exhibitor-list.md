## **ABOUT CEMENT INDUSTRY** 

- INDIA’S CEMENT INDUSTRY, the second largest in the world. 

- The industry will add 80-100 million tonnes (mt) of capacity by 2024-25 (FY25). 

- Input costs which have hurt the bottomlines are beginning to cool down. 

- The biggest innovation efforts are directed towards decarbonisation and logistics. 

- Procurement will undergo some shift and so will logistics.
