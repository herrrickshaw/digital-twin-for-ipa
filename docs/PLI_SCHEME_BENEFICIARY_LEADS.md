## ⚠️ CORRECTIONS THAT AFFECT EVERY PITCH

**1. The ISM subsidy rate is 25–35%, and falling — not 50%, never 70%.**
30% (Cabinet, Dec 2021) → 50% uniform (Sept 2022) → **25–35% under Semicon 2.0 today.** Micron's 50% was the earlier regime. Quoting 50% to a prospect who then discovers 25–35% destroys the conversation.

**2. PARIVESH is a null instrument for electronics/semiconductors** (may still work for chemicals/metals/heavy industry). 29,892 central EC proposals scanned, zero hits for known-real projects like Tata Electronics or CG Power. Semiconductor and electronics assembly don't route through central EC.

**3. Approval ≠ money, everywhere in this file.** PLI-Auto: ₹1,350.83cr disbursed of ₹25,938cr outlay. PLI-LSEM/Telecom: partial. PLI Solar, NGHM/SIGHT, PLI-ACC Battery, Bulk Drug Parks: **zero disbursed/zero tenants** as of the latest tabled answer. Treat "approved" as a qualifying signal, never as deployed capital, unless a disbursement figure is stated.

**4. No Indian government scheme publishes an ownership/parent-company field.** ALMM, MNRE, SECI, MHI, MeitY, DoP — none of them. Every foreign-parent attribution in this file is a cross-reference to the company's own filings/website, clearly marked, not a government-stated fact.
